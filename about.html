About us

Design that means something

FOCUS360 STUDIO is a creative design studio in Ghana, working with local and international brands.

Our story

Why we exist

Too many brands look the same, say the same things and fade into the background.

FOCUS360 STUDIO was built to change that. We are a creative studio in Ghana that combines strategy, craft and bold ideas to give brands a presence people notice and remember.

The name says how we work: we look at a brand from every angle, 360 degrees, then focus on the one idea that makes it different. From a logo to a full campaign, every decision has a reason.

Our creative philosophy

We believe in three things

01

Design communicates

Beautiful is not enough. Design has to say the right thing, to the right person, in a second.

02

Ideas connect

People remember how a brand makes them feel. We look for the idea that creates that connection.

03

Detail earns trust

Typography, spacing, colour and finish. Careful craft shows a brand takes itself, and its customers, seriously.

Our mission

Make brands clear

To help brands in Ghana and beyond communicate clearly, connect emotionally and stand out through design that works as hard as it looks.

Our vision

Known for bold work

To be a Ghanaian creative studio recognised around the world for thoughtful, confident and original design.
